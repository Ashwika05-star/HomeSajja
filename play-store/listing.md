# Play Store listing text (copy into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing)

**App name (max 30):** `HomeSajja: Furniture Reuse`

**Short description (max 80):**
`Buy, sell, swap, repair and recycle furniture with trusted local vendors.`

**Full description (max 4000):**

HomeSajja gives furniture a second life. Whether you're clearing out a room, hunting for a bargain, or wondering if that wobbly chair is worth fixing, HomeSajja brings everything together in one warm, simple app, built for Indian homes and cities.

**Buy and sell**
Browse pre-loved sofas, beds, tables, wardrobes and more in your city, all priced in rupees. List your own furniture in a few taps with photos, condition and price. Save favourites, chat with sellers, make an offer, and pay through Google Pay (UPI) when you're ready.

**Exchange**
Not looking for cash? Swap your furniture for something you actually want.

**Repair**
Describe the damage, add photos, and send a request to repair professionals and carpenters near you. Follow every stage: Requested, Accepted, In progress, Ready, Completed.

**Recycle**
Beyond repair? Choose pickup or drop-off with a local recycler, and follow it through to completion.

**Local vendors you can trust**
Shops, carpenters, refurbishers and recyclers have their own profiles with location on a map, catalogue, ratings and reviews. Look for the Verified badge.

**Ask HomeSajja and Sajja AI**
Not sure whether to sell, repair, exchange or recycle? Add a photo and get a friendly suggestion, with a price range in rupees. Chat with Sajja AI to find furniture or get advice. It is always just a suggestion: you decide.

**Chat and notifications**
Message buyers, sellers and vendors in real time and get updates on your requests.

**Safe by design**
Report a listing or person, block anyone who bothers you, leave honest reviews, and delete your account any time from your profile.

**For vendors**
A dashboard with your listings, incoming requests, material requests and a public shop profile, all in one place.

**Available in:** Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru, Delhi and Hyderabad.
