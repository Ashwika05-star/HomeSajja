HomeSajja Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

HomeSajja ("we", "the app") is a marketplace for buying, selling, exchanging, repairing and recycling furniture in India. This policy explains what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

Account details you give us: your name, email address, phone number and the city you choose when you sign up. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them.

Vendor details (vendor accounts only): business name and type, description, shop address, the shop location you pick on a map, and brochure or catalogue photos.

What you post and do in the app: listing photos, titles, descriptions and prices; purchase, exchange, repair, recycling and material requests; chat messages; reviews and ratings; saved items; reports and blocks; and notifications about your activity. If you are a seller, the UPI ID you type when accepting a sale is stored with that request so the buyer can pay you.

Your phone: a push-notification token, so we can alert you about messages and requests.

Location: we do not use GPS. You choose a city. Vendors choose a shop location on a map. Showing the map means your phone requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap, which sees your IP address.

We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your data.

How we use it

To run the marketplace: show your listings to people in your city, connect buyers, sellers and vendors, deliver messages and notifications, show ratings, and keep the community safe (reports and blocks).

AI features

If you use "Ask HomeSajja" or "Sajja AI", the photo and text you provide, and your city, are sent to Google's Gemini service through Firebase to generate a suggestion. AI suggestions are only advice. Don't include anything sensitive in these requests.

Who else handles your data

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, Cloud Messaging, AI Logic/Gemini): stores account data, content and messages, and delivers notifications.
  • Cloudinary: stores and delivers the photos you upload.
  • OpenStreetMap: map tiles for shop locations.
  • Google Pay or another UPI app: if you choose to pay, you complete payment inside that app under its own terms. HomeSajja does not process or see your payment credentials, and cannot confirm a payment; buyers and sellers mark payments as done themselves.

Other users see what you choose to make public: your listings, your public profile (vendors) or name and reviews (people), and messages you send them. Your email, phone number and city are not shown to other users.

Keeping it safe

Data travels over encrypted connections. Access to stored data is restricted by security rules so people can only read and change what they should. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can delete your account inside the app: Profile → Delete my account. This removes your sign-in, your profile, your listings, saved items, blocks, notifications and (for vendors) your material requests. Records you share with another person, such as chats, requests and the reviews you wrote, stay so the other person keeps their history. If you also want those removed, email us.

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing ashwikasinha27@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. You may also report content or block people from inside the app.

Children

HomeSajja is not meant for children under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
